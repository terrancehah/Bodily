# Bodily — Design Context

## Users
A single athlete (the developer) glancing at Garmin recovery metrics on their macOS desktop throughout the day. The widget is a passive instrument; the host app is a control surface (login, refresh, account). The job to be done: answer "how recovered am I right now?" in under 2 seconds.

## Brand Personality
Athletic, precise, confident. 3 words: **driven, measured, volt**. The interface should feel like a sports instrument — data-forward, no decoration that doesn't convey state. Emotion: quiet confidence, not anxiety.

## Aesthetic Direction
Athletic data-dense (Garmin Connect / Strava energy) with an adaptive light/dark theme.

### Palette (brand colors)
- **Ink** `#000000` — darkest base. Never use pure black for large surfaces; tint toward slate for surfaces, keep ink for maximum-contrast moments.
- **Slate** `#5d737e` — the neutral spine. All neutrals (surfaces, secondary text, borders) are tinted toward this blue-grey hue. Secondary text is a lightened slate, never plain gray.
- **Volt** `#ffe313` — the single accent. Used sparingly: hero highlights, gauge tracks that are "full", refresh/active indicators, key CTAs. Metric zone colors (green/yellow/orange/red) remain for status values but are tuned to harmonize with volt.
- Adaptive: dark mode = near-black slate-tinted surfaces with volt accents; light mode = off-white slate-tinted surfaces with the same accent. Both must look intentional, not inverted.

### Typography
System rounded is kept for warmth but hierarchy gets more contrast: bold tabular-feeling numerals for values, small-caps tracked labels for section headers and tile labels. At least 1.25 ratio between type steps. Values are the heroes — labels stay quiet.

### Shape & Detail
- No decorative cards-in-cards; hairline slate borders over tinted fills.
- Every metric gets a thin track/gauge bar showing position in its 0–100 zone — the signature element. Track is slate at low opacity; fill is the zone color; volt marks excellence.
- Generous but rhythmic spacing on a 4pt scale.

## Design Principles
1. **Values are the heroes** — numbers lead, labels support, decoration earns its place.
2. **One accent** — volt yellow is rare and therefore powerful; zone colors carry status.
3. **Slate, not gray** — every neutral is tinted toward #5d737e for subconscious cohesion.
4. **Gauges over text** — where a number can also be a position on a track, show the track.
5. **Adaptive by design** — light and dark are both first-class, tuned separately.
